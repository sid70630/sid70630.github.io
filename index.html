Siddharth Doshi

PhD researcher in nuclear physics · University of Brighton

I study the structure of neutron-rich nuclei through β–γ spectroscopy, lifetime measurements and isomeric states.

My PhD research focuses on neutron-rich hafnium and tantalum isotopes studied at KISS, RIKEN. I also analyse storage-ring data from GSI for mass and half-life measurements. My work combines experimental data analysis with nuclear-structure interpretation.

Research

At KISS, I analyse decay and timing data to identify delayed γ-ray transitions and isomeric states in neutron-rich nuclei. My first-author paper reports evidence for a π1/2⁺[411] isomeric state in ¹⁸³Ta with a half-life of 41+6−4 μs.

For storage-ring data from GSI, I develop Python workflows for particle identification, frequency-spectrum analysis and multi-parameter fitting. I use ROOT, Radware, Python and C++ in my spectroscopy and analysis work.
