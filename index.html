Siddharth Doshi

PhD researcher at the University of Brighton

I study the structure of neutron-rich nuclei through β–γ spectroscopy, lifetime measurements and isomeric states. My PhD focuses on hafnium and tantalum isotopes studied at KISS, RIKEN. I also analyse storage-ring data from GSI for mass and half-life measurements.
