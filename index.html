About

My PhD research focuses on neutron-rich hafnium and tantalum isotopes studied at KISS, RIKEN. I analyse decay and timing data to investigate isomeric states and the structure of low-lying nuclear levels.

I also work with storage-ring data from GSI for mass and half-life measurements, and have contributed to nuclear-physics experiments at several international facilities.

Research

Decay spectroscopy and isomers

Using β–γ timing correlations and γ-ray spectroscopy, I study delayed transitions in neutron-rich hafnium and tantalum. My first-author paper reports evidence for a π1/2⁺[411] isomeric state in ¹⁸³Ta with a half-life of 41+6−4 μs.

Storage-ring analysis

I develop Python workflows for event-by-event data, particle identification, frequency-spectrum analysis and multi-parameter fitting for mass and half-life studies.

Tools: Python, ROOT, Radware, C++, Linux and LaTeX.
