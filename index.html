01 / About

Looking inside the nucleus

I study neutron-rich nuclei far from stability, with a focus on how their excited states and decays reveal nuclear structure.

My PhD work combines β–γ spectroscopy and lifetime measurements with the interpretation of isomeric states in hafnium and tantalum. I work across experimental data analysis, detector systems and nuclear-structure calculations.

PhD in Nuclear Physics · University of Brighton, United Kingdom

02 / Research

Current work

KISS · RIKEN

Neutron-rich Hf and Ta

Analysis of β–γ spectroscopy and lifetime data from multinucleon transfer experiments, including delayed γ rays and isomeric states in tantalum.

ESR · GSI

Storage-ring analysis

Python-based analysis of event-by-event data, particle identification and frequency spectra for mass and half-life studies of neutron-rich nuclei.

Interpretation

Nuclear structure

Nilsson configurations, transition strengths and systematics of neighbouring nuclei help connect observed decay patterns to underlying states.

Instrumentation

Detectors and DAQ

Hands-on work with HPGe detectors, plastic scintillators with SiPM readout, electronics, calibration and experimental setups.
